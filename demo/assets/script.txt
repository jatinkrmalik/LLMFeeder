Welcome to LLMFeeder — the fastest way to turn any webpage into clean, LLM-ready Markdown.

Whether you're prompting ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI assistant, LLMFeeder gives you perfectly formatted context in one click.

Here's how it works. Open any article or documentation page.
Then click the LLMFeeder icon in your toolbar.

Want the main article only? Choose Main Content.
Need everything on the page? Switch to Full Page.
Or convert just what you've selected.

Hit Convert and Copy — and your Markdown is instantly on the clipboard, ready to paste into your favorite LLM.

Prefer a file? Download Markdown instead, or grab a ZIP when you're working across multiple tabs.

Need fine-tuned control? Open Settings to toggle images, tables, links, titles, and custom metadata templates.
There's even a live token counter so you know if your content fits your model's context window.

Privacy first: everything runs locally in your browser. No servers. No tracking. Your content never leaves your device.
