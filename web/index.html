Measured with this repository's own readability.js and turndown.js on MDN's animation-timeline page (100,238 to 8,577 est. tokens). Across five pages: median 10.4× reduction (4.5× to 15.1×). Estimates use the extension's own ~4 bytes/token heuristic, not a billed tokenizer.
